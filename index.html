      Usage guidelines

      • Accent identifies the single preferred action in a view or workflow step.
      • Default supports regular actions that do not need primary emphasis.
      • Ghost is for secondary, low-emphasis actions in compact toolbars.
      • Destructive primary belongs in explicit confirmation flows.
      • Destructive ghost suits compact row and toolbar actions.
