Integration contract

Scheduler measures, trip bars own height

Placement

  • Trips are placed from date math: day start, span, and lane.
  • Normal trips sit var(--rux-space-2) away from day grid lines.
  • Selected trip rings paint outward into that inset, leaving a visible var(--rux-space-1) gap.
  • Week continuations use fromPrev or toNext and flush to the rail edge.

Row growth

  • Each trip bar keeps its own collapsed, selected, or expanded height.
  • A ResizeObserver measures visible bars and updates --_row-height.
  • Stacked lanes add each lane height plus var(--rux-space-2) lane gaps.

Data coverage

  • Includes single-day, multi-day, paid, pending, and conflict states.
  • Includes pending PO, pending contract, and pending contact icons.
  • Includes TBA drivers and customer trip bus grouping.
